# AdaSouls DRep: conflict of interest policy
 
AdaSouls builds identity, spending limits and reputation for AI agents, builds ALDEA World, runs the SOULS stake pool, and may apply for treasury or Catalyst funding. Those interests can meet our votes, so we say how we handle them.
 
## We vote Abstain, and say why, when an action
 
- funds, pays or otherwise benefits AdaSouls, its founders or its staff;
- funds ALDEA World or any other project AdaSouls builds;
- funds a project in which AdaSouls is a partner, contractor or vendor;
- funds a direct competitor of AdaSouls, such as another agent identity or agent payments network.
  
## We vote, and disclose our interest, when an action
 
- changes stake pool economics for every operator, such as `minPoolCost`. We run SOULS and say so in the rationale.
- sets the budget for Catalyst as a whole while AdaSouls has proposals in it. We list those proposals in the rationale.
  
## We publish
 
- every treasury or Catalyst proposal AdaSouls submits or joins;
- any partnership or payment that could matter to a vote;
- every vote with its rationale and the analysis behind it, in `votes/`.
  
## Our SDK
 
The AdaSouls SDK never delegates anyone's vote to this DRep by default. Its default is always-abstain; developers can choose this DRep, or any other, explicitly.
